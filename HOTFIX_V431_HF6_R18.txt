Westcon Decision Intelligence v4.3.1-HF6-R18
Gap-aware quality accounting

Root cause:
The system has two distinct concepts:
1. integrity defects: unsupported or malformed facts that escaped the control model;
2. research/coverage debt: facts deliberately preserved while an explicit research gap remains open.

After strict atomic provenance was introduced, engine.quality penalised both concepts identically.
As a result, normal open research debt drove quality_score to the hard floor of 80 while
quality errors remained empty. scripts/validate.py then rejected the Daily because score < 95.

R18 keeps the 95 threshold and all hard gates. It does NOT suppress genuine unsupported facts.
A missing-evidence fact is exempt from a quality warning only when research_gaps.json contains
an exact matching open gap for the same section/entity/field and, for atomic items, the same item.
Wrong or absent gaps do not suppress the warning.

The installer also performs an end-to-end canonical rebuild in a disposable Git worktree and runs
scripts/validate.py against the rebuilt result before allowing the patch to remain installed.
No Daily/Weekly/Monthly web research is run.
